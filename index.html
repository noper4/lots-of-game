QUICK GUIDE

Make a little chaos.

  • Drag any body part to fling the ragdoll.
  • New ragdoll or press R to add another.
  • Big shove or press Space for a surprise.
  • Pause or press P to freeze the action.

Tip: you can play with multiple ragdolls at once.
